# Account security check

## Goal
Harden my own accounts and document what I changed.

## What I did
- Turned on 2FA for email
- Turned on 2FA for GitHub
- Saved recovery codes somewhere offline
- Checked that my main passwords are unique

## What I learned
2FA blocks most account takeovers even if a password leaks. Recovery codes matter if I lose the phone.

## Next
Repeat this for bank and social accounts.
